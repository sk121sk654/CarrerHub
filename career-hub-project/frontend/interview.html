Interview Preparation Hub

Practice with common interview questions and answers.

💻 Technical Questions

🔧

What is a closure in JavaScript?

A closure is a function that remembers its outer variables even after the outer function has returned.
🗄️

Explain normalization in databases.

Normalization reduces redundancy and improves integrity in databases by organizing data efficiently.
🧮

Difference between == and ===?

== compares values loosely, === checks type and value (strict equality).
📡

Explain REST vs GraphQL

REST uses fixed endpoints; GraphQL lets clients query exactly what they need.
🧱

Four pillars of OOP?

Encapsulation, Abstraction, Inheritance, Polymorphism.
📐

What is binary search?

A divide-and-conquer algorithm to find elements in sorted arrays. O(log n).

🧠 HR Questions

👤

Tell me about yourself.

Start with a brief intro, your education, skills, and career goals relevant to the role.
⚖️

What are your strengths and weaknesses?

Share a genuine strength with examples and a weakness you're improving.
🔮

Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

Discuss your goals aligned with growth and learning in the company.
🎯

Why should we hire you?

Highlight your unique skills, adaptability, and passion for the role.
🤝

How do you handle team conflict?

Share an example of resolving conflict with communication and understanding.
📉

Tell me about a time you failed.

Explain the situation, what you learned, and how it helped you grow.
